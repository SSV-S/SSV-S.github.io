About Me

I'm a Detection, SIEM, and SOAR focused Security Consultant based in Dallas, TX - designing detections, incident response playbooks, and automation workflows that help SOC teams move faster with better context.

My SIEM work starts with telemetry physics: source normalization, index hygiene, field extraction, data-model acceleration, CIM alignment, and risk-based alerting. From there I build SPL correlation searches, dashboards, notable-event workflows, and ATT&CK-mapped detections that are measurable, tunable, and defensible during incident review.

My SOAR engineering connects the detection plane to the action plane: enrichment, deduplication, containment, ticketing, analyst prompts, case notes, and closed-loop feedback. I design playbooks so automation is not theatrical button-clicking; it is a controlled response graph with guardrails, rollback paths, and human approval where the blast radius matters.

As a detection engineer, I care about signal epistemology: what the alert claims, which evidence supports it, what benign mechanisms explain it, and how the logic fails. That is where the pseudo-profound bullshit principle becomes useful in practice - if a detection cannot be tested, tuned, explained, or mapped to adversary behavior, it is probably just impressive noise.
